## Description

Jean is the wife of Tobias, the baker of Oakhaven. Together with her husband, she helped nurse Fronn back to health when he arrived at their village half-dead and emaciated.

## Relationship with Party

Cared for Fronn during his recovery in Oakhaven. He left to protect her family from danger.
