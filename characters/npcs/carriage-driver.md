# Carriage Driver (unnamed)

**Status:** Captured (crated by the party)
**Attitude:** Hostile (uncooperative)
**First met:** Session-001

**Race:** Human | **Class:** Commoner
**Role:** Courier for Skyanite shippers

---

## Description

An ordinary-looking carriage driver who was transporting a large quantity of Skyanite to the Adventurer's Guild in Vaelorian. He initially claimed the cargo was secret and not to be pried into. Under threat from Varesh, he confessed it was Skyanite but refused to give further details about who hired him or why.

He was stuffed into an empty crate by Varesh and remains captive with the party.

## Relationship with Party

Hostile and uncooperative. Knows more than he is telling.

## Notes

- Works for the unknown party shipping Skyanite to Vaelorian
- Currently crated and travelling with the party toward Vaelorian
- His true employers are unknown
