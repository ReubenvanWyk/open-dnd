# Samira Ashkar

**Status:** Alive (whereabouts unknown)
**Attitude:** Friendly (to Varesh)
**First met:** Background (pre-campaign)

**Race:** Human | **Class:** [Unknown]
**Role:** Varesh's older sister

---

## Description

Samira was everything Varesh was not: more open, more rebellious, more willing to question the traditions her family followed. She loved the Sunspears but believed the Order had lost sight of the people it existed to protect. She saw how Karim's teachings had changed — how fear had replaced compassion.

She eventually decided to leave, not because she hated the Sunspears but because she still believed in what they were supposed to be.

## Key Events

- Tried to flee the family under cover of darkness
- Karim confronted her; their argument escalated to violence
- Karim wounded her with his spear, leaving a deep scar along her left arm
- Escaped into the desert
- Karim claimed she had been killed by wolves
- Varesh witnessed everything and told no one

## Notes

- Only Varesh and Karim know she survived
- Formerly close with Marshal Cassian Jaleel, who believes her dead
- Her fate is one of the campaign's unresolved threads
