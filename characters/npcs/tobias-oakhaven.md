# Tobias

**Status:** Alive
**Attitude:** Friendly
**First met:** Background (pre-campaign)

**Race:** Human | **Class:** Commoner (Baker)
**Role:** Baker of Oakhaven

---

## Description

Tobias is the local baker of Oakhaven. He and his wife Jean took in a half-dead Fronn after he stumbled into their village. They tended his infected wounds and fed him without asking intrusive questions.

Tobias is a kind, trusting man who judges people by their actions rather than their past. Fronn worked in his bakery for nearly a year, learning the trade and finding peace.

## Relationship with Party

Fronn left Oakhaven in the night to protect Tobias and his family from the slave-catchers pursuing him. He left a heartfelt letter of gratitude and farewell.

## Notes

- Married to Jean
- Father of Mina
- Knows Fronn as "Yeast" — the quiet, strong baker
- Does not know Fronn's true history as a gladiator
