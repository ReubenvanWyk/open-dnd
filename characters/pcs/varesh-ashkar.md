## Personality

**Personality Traits:**
- I rarely speak first; I listen, observe, then offer my thoughts quietly.
- I apologise far too often, even when something is not my fault.
- I instinctively stand closest to the door in any room.
- When travelling, I unconsciously count my companions every few minutes.
- I always sharpen my spear before sleeping.

**Ideals:**
- **Mercy.** Justice without mercy becomes vengeance.
- **Protection.** The strength of a spear is not what it pierces, but what it prevents from being harmed.
- **Duty.** A Sunspear's greatest duty is preventing suffering before it happens.

**Bonds:**
- House Ashkar — my family name is both a blessing and a burden I must redeem.
- The Sunspear Order — I love it, even as I fear what it has become.
- Samira — my sister, whom I failed by staying silent.

**Flaws:**
- I carry secrets to protect others, even when the truth would serve them better.
- I believe enduring hardship silently is what heroes do.
- I can understand both sides of any argument, which sometimes paralyses me.

## Backstory

Varesh Ashkar was born into House Ashkar, an ancient Sunspear noble family from Vabbi known for generations of commanders, historians, and Paragons. As a Kalashtar, she experienced vivid dreams of Kormir and Varesh Ossa — one a goddess of sacrifice and hope, the other a figure of betrayal and corruption. Her father, Karim Ashkar, gave her the name Varesh deliberately, believing a troubled legacy could be redeemed through honourable deeds.

Her mother Nadijeh taught that strength existed to protect others through compassion. Her father Karim believed duty sometimes demanded harsh decisions to prevent greater suffering. Her older sister Samira rejected their father's worldview. The night Samira tried to flee, Karim confronted her and wounded her with his spear. He told the Sunspears she had been killed by wolves. Varesh, who witnessed everything, said nothing. This became the first great secret of her life.

Determined to restore honour to the Ashkar name, Varesh devoted herself to the Sunspears and became an exceptional Paragon. She earned a reputation for balancing discipline with genuine compassion, advocating for mercy, and protecting ordinary people. But small compromises began to accumulate. A prisoner she spared escaped and destroyed a settlement, killing her mentor. She began to question the Order she served.

During an expedition into forgotten Elonian ruins, Varesh discovered a Bloodstone — forbidden knowledge that the gods deemed too dangerous for mortals. She lied in her report, saying nothing important was found. Knowing the Sunspears would eventually discover the truth, she left the Order to search for answers, travelling with a group of misfits who did not know her reputation.
