# Mina

**Status:** Alive
**Attitude:** Friendly
**First met:** Background (pre-campaign)

**Race:** Human | **Class:** Commoner
**Role:** Tobias and Jean's daughter

---

## Description

Mina is the young daughter of Tobias and Jean of Oakhaven. She was picking wildflowers when she found a half-dead Fronn on the outskirts of the village. Her striking innocence reminded Fronn so fiercely of his own daughter Elara that he broke down.

She offered him water and fetched her father, setting in motion the chain of events that led to Fronn's year of peace in Oakhaven.

## Relationship with Party

Instrumental in saving Fronn's life. He left Oakhaven to protect her and her family.

## Notes

- Kind-hearted and trusting
- Her resemblance to Elara affected Fronn deeply
