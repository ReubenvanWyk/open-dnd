# Admiral Zahir al-Marin

**Status:** Alive
**Attitude:** Respectful (becoming distrustful)
**First met:** Background (pre-campaign)

**Race:** Human | **Class:** Naval Commander
**Role:** Admiral of the Istani Royal Navy

---

## Description

Admiral Zahir al-Marin is a naval commander of the Istani Royal Navy. He respects Varesh's dedication and skill as a Sunspear Paragon, but later grows suspicious of her Bloodstone research.

The Istani Royal Navy protects trade routes, coastal villages, and defends against Corsairs. They are natural allies of the Sunspears.

## Relationship with Party

Initially respects Varesh. As rumours of the Bloodstone spread, his trust wavers.

## Notes

- Commands the Istani Royal Navy
- Values honour and transparency
- Represents the traditional Sunspear-ally viewpoint
