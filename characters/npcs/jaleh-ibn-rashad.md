# Jaleh ibn Rashad

**Status:** Alive
**Attitude:** Friendly
**First met:** Background (pre-campaign)

**Race:** Human | **Class:** Noble / Scholar
**Role:** Youngest son of a Vabbian merchant-prince family

---

## Description

Jaleh is the youngest son of one of Vabbi's wealthy merchant-prince families, whose fortune was built on caravans, libraries, and trade across Elona. Unlike most of his siblings who pursued politics and commerce, Jaleh developed a fascination with history, music, and the people who protected the realm rather than ruled it.

He enjoys fine clothes, extravagant dinners, poetry competitions, and the latest Vabbian theatre productions. He has an uncanny ability to convince Varesh to accompany him despite her insistence that she has "more important things to do." He delights in teasing her stoic nature.

Unlike many nobles, Jaleh has no desire for military glory. He believes diplomacy, art, and culture preserve kingdoms just as effectively as armies.

## Relationship with Party

Met Varesh through diplomatic gatherings between his family and the Sunspears when they were teenagers. They formed an unlikely friendship that continues to the present.

## Notes

- Exceptionally perceptive — notices when Varesh is exhausted before anyone else
- One of the few people willing to tell Varesh she carries responsibilities that don't belong to her
- Has little patience for self-sacrifice that becomes self-destruction
- First to notice Varesh's growing darkness: "She has stopped laughing at my terrible jokes"
