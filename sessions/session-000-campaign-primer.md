## Campaign Overview

**Campaign Name:** The Spear That Carried the Sun
**Setting / Genre:** Elona — Guild Wars 1: Nightfall setting adapted to D&D 5.5e
**Tone:** Heroic fantasy with moral complexity
**Starting Region:** Istan
**Starting Location:** Kamadan, Jewel of Istan

## The World

The campaign is set in Elona, a sun-baked continent divided into three provinces: the island of Istan (heroic, naval, birthplace of the Sunspears), the military stronghold of Kourna (discipline, order, sacrifice), and the wealthy scholarly province of Vabbi (art, knowledge, commerce). Beyond them lies the Desolation, a corrupted wasteland shaped by the fallen god Abaddon.

The Order of the Sunspears, an ancient military-religious order, has protected Elona for centuries. But the world is changing. Forbidden knowledge stirs beneath forgotten ruins. The line between protector and tyrant grows thin.

**Important homebrew rules setting changes that override RAW 5.5e:**
- The Oath of the Paragon is used as a paladin Sacred Oath option (see `homebrew/oath-of-the-paragon.md`)
- Elonian provinces have distinct governments and cultures that affect local reception and reputation
- Bloodstones exist as artifacts of forbidden knowledge and power, carrying both mechanical benefits and narrative corruption

## Starting Situation

The campaign follows Varesh Ashkar, a young Kalashtar Paladin of the Sunspears who has recently discovered a Bloodstone beneath ancient Elonian ruins. She lied in her report to the Order, claiming nothing important was found.

Knowing the truth will eventually surface, Varesh has left the Sunspear command structure — not as a betrayal, but as a search for answers. She travels with a small group of misfits: a former Corsair captain, a scholar of forgotten civilizations, a thief who admits his flaws rather than hiding them behind honour, and a former Sunspear who already did what Varesh fears most — left.

The party begins in Kamadan, where rumours of Bloodstone research are beginning to spread. The Sunspears still consider Varesh one of their own — for now. The Order of Whispers watches. The gods remain silent. And somewhere in the desert, a figure from Varesh's past may still be alive.
