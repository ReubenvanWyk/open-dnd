## Backstory

### Act I: The Wardens of Skyvein

In the rugged peaks of the Northern Crests lay Skyvein Hollow, a small mountain chiefdom blessed with a rare light-turquoise mineral known as Skyanite. Prized by archmages and artificers for its ability to channel raw magic, the ore formed a symbiotic invisible barrier around the hollow that repelled hostile intent. As chief, Fronn Heartshield enforced strict conservation laws, mining only what they needed.

His beloved wife Rowena died giving birth to their daughter Elara. Fronn raised Elara while leading his people, wearing the family name Heartshield as a promise to protect those he cherished.

### Act II: The Hostage

The Iron-Blood Vanguard, a brutal warband of mountain raiders, devised a scheme to claim the Skyanite. Unable to breach the symbiotic barrier, they ambushed 11-year-old Elara outside the ward's border. They demanded Fronn's surrender in exchange for his daughter's life. Without hesitation, Fronn handed leadership to his sister and mother, entrusted Elara to their care, and surrendered himself.

For months the Vanguard tortured him to reveal the barrier's secret. Fronn told the truth: the barrier was a living reflection of the heart — it allowed entry only to those without malice. Realising they could not force the mountain to yield, the warband sold Fronn to underground slave traders.

### Act III: The Pits

For six years, Fronn survived in blood-soaked gladiator arenas. Forced to kill or be killed, he used his strength and defensive instincts to dominate every match, earning the title "Redfist." He despised every victory. Six years after his capture, Fronn seized an opportunity during an arena transport ambush, grabbed a war pick and shield, and escaped.

For a year he fled through wilderness, disoriented and alone, surviving on scavenged roots and wild game, travelling only at night.

### Act IV: The Baker of Oakhaven

Near total exhaustion, Fronn stumbled into Oakhaven, a peaceful valley village. There he met Mina, a young girl picking wildflowers. Her innocence reminded him so fiercely of Elara that he broke down. Mina's father Tobias, the local baker, took Fronn in.

Fronn lied, claiming his home had been destroyed by raiders. To repay his debt, he worked at the bakery. His immense strength made kneading dough effortless, and his quiet presence endeared him to the townsfolk. For nearly a year he lived as "Fronn the Quiet Baker" — known by the name "Yeast."

Through discrete inquiries, he finally learned Skyvein Hollow's location: thousands of miles away, across dangerous lands.

### Act V: Forced into the Shadows

Fronn's peace ended when wealthy travellers visited Oakhaven. Among them were gamblers who recognised the former star gladiator. Knowing slave-catchers would soon follow, Fronn packed his savings, draped his animal furs, left a farewell letter on the bakery counter, and vanished into the night — now an adventurer with a clear goal: raise funds, navigate the perilous road home, and reunite with Elara.
