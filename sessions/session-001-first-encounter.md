# Session 001 — The Road to Vaelorian

**Date:** 2026-07-22
**Players:** Marian (Varesh), Jada (Fronn)
**Session Duration:** 19:00 — 22:30

## Previously…

This was the first session. Varesh Ashkar was a Sunspear Paragon secretly investigating a Bloodstone she had discovered. Fronn Heartshield was a former gladiator and chief fleeing slave-catchers, trying to find his way home to Skyvein Hollow.

---

## The Session

### Scene 1: The Road to Vaelorian

Varesh, Idris, and Liyana were on an escort mission carrying a "secret" cargo to the Adventurer's Guild in Vaelorian. The carriage driver warned them not to pry into the contents.

Fronn was camped nearby and heard the carriage approaching. He went to investigate, but stumbled, alerting the group. Idris moved to investigate the noise while Varesh and Liyana drew weapons.

**Key Events:**
- Escort mission for a mysterious cargo destined for Vaelorian's Adventurer's Guild
- Fronn discovered attempting to observe the caravan
- Party split: Idris scouting, Varesh and Liyana guarding the carriage

**NPCs Introduced:**
- Carriage driver (unnamed, suspicious, works for the Skyanite shippers)

**Decisions Made:**
- The party chose to investigate rather than attack the hidden figure immediately

### Scene 2: Bandit Attack

Just before Idris reached Fronn's position, a group of 3 bandits attacked the carriage. Idris confronted Fronn instead of engaging the bandits immediately. Fronn asked if they needed help; Idris accepted.

The group fought together — two bandits were slain (one by Idris, one by Varesh) and the third surrendered.

The surviving bandit was questioned. He was from a village in the outskirts of Vaelorian, nothing more than a common highway robber.

**Combat / Encounters:**
- 3 bandits vs. Varesh, Idris, Liyana, Fronn
- 2 bandits killed, 1 captured and questioned
- XP awarded

**NPCs Introduced:**
- Bandit scout (unnamed, from Vaelorian outskirts, surrendered)

### Scene 3: The Skyanite Reveal

Varesh noticed the carriage driver acting suspicious and threatened him to reveal the cargo. He confessed it was a large quantity of Skyanite — a magical crystal.

Fronn, recognising the crystal from his homeland Skyvein Hollow, broke open the side of the cart to confirm. The group could not get further answers from the driver. Varesh stuffed him into one of the empty crates.

During the search, Fronn found a Devil Mask in the carriage and kept it. Varesh and her companions assumed it was a normal carnival mask.

**Key Events:**
- Cargo revealed as Skyanite — connecting Fronn's past to the present plot
- Carriage driver crated and contained
- Devil Mask recovered from the cargo

**Decisions Made:**
- Party decided to take the carriage and cargo to Vaelorian together
- Fronn kept the Devil Mask without revealing its potential significance

### Scene 4: Arrival at Vaelorian

The group travelled together for 2 days toward Vaelorian. During the journey, Fronn grew suspicious of Varesh, mistakenly believing her commanding demeanour and Sunspear authority might be connected to the slave trade that had once enslaved him.

They arrived at Vaelorian's gate at dusk. Guards were preparing to inspect them and their cargo.

**Key Events:**
- 2 days of uneasy travel together
- Fronn's suspicion of Varesh grows (misinterpretation of her authority)
- Party arrives at Vaelorian's gate at dusk

**Decisions Made:**
- Party approached the gate openly with the carriage (intending to bluff or negotiate entry)

## Session Summary
- **Major plot advancement:** Skyanite enters the campaign as a central mcguffin; Fronn and Varesh's parties converge
- **Unresolved threads:** Identity of the Skyanite shippers; what awaits at the Adventurer's Guild; the guards at Vaelorian's gate; the Devil Mask's true nature
- **Player highlights:** Fronn breaking the crate open on instinct upon seeing Skyanite; Varesh intimidating the driver into confessing
