# Nadijeh Ashkar

**Status:** Alive
**Attitude:** Friendly
**First met:** Background (pre-campaign)

**Race:** Human | **Class:** [Unknown]
**Role:** Varesh's mother, House Ashkar matriarch

---

## Description

Nadijeh Ashkar believes that the purpose of strength is compassion. She taught Varesh that mercy is not weakness and that even the smallest acts of kindness can preserve someone's humanity.

Her philosophy: "Even the desert blooms when someone remembers to water it."

She taught Varesh traditional Elonian calligraphy, which became Varesh's greatest hobby.

## Relationship with Party

Varesh's mother. The only person who ever called Varesh "Little Sun."

## Notes

- Used to hum a traditional Sunspear funeral hymn while braiding Varesh's hair
- After Samira disappeared, she never sang it again
- Taught strength through compassion and mercy, in contrast to Karim's harsh discipline
