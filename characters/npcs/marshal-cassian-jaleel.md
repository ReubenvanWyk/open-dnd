# Marshal Cassian Jaleel

**Status:** Alive
**Attitude:** Friendly
**First met:** Background (pre-campaign)

**Race:** Human | **Class:** Sunspear Commander

## Description

Cassian Jaleel is a respected Sunspear commander who served as both mentor and trusted commander to Varesh. Their relationship was founded on mutual respect rather than romance, though others often mistook their closeness for something more.

Before Varesh's time, Cassian had been close with Samira Ashkar. He believed the official account that she had died beyond the village and mourned her quietly. He never spoke of his feelings, instead burying himself in service to the Sunspears. Varesh watched this from a distance, carrying the knowledge that he grieved someone who was still alive.

## Relationship with Party

Childhood crush of Varesh Ashkar. Their bond is built on years of shared campaigns, quiet conversations after battle, and a trust that neither fully understands.
