## Campaign Overview

**Campaign Name:** *TBD*

**Tone:** Heroic fantasy with moral complexity

**Starting Location:** A caravan on the road to Vaelorian

## The World

Elona is a land of sun-scorched deserts, ancient dynasties, and divine purpose. The Sunspear Order maintains vigil against supernatural threats while noble houses, merchant princes, and military councils vie for influence. In the distant Northern Crests, the mineral Skyanite forms symbiotic barriers around mountain chiefdoms. Beneath it all, forbidden knowledge — Bloodstones, Abaddon's legacy, hidden histories — waits to be unearthed.

**Important homebrew rules / setting changes:**
- **Oath of the Paragon** — custom paladin subclass (Sunspear-inspired commander)
- **Skyanite** — a light-turquoise mineral that forms symbiotic anti-hostility barriers; central campaign macguffin
- **Kalashtar race** (from Eberron) — official race used for Varesh Ashkar
- Custom factions with Guild Wars flavour (House Ashkar, Astral Scholars, Order of Whispers, Iron-Blood Vanguard, etc.)
- Setting pulls from Guild Wars lore (Elona, Sunspears, Abaddon) mixed with D&D 5.5e mechanics

## Starting Situation

Two converging threads:

1. **Varesh Ashkar** — a Kalashtar Sunspear Paragon. She discovered a forbidden Bloodstone in ancient ruins and lied in her report. Now she travels with Captain Idris and Liyana on an escort mission carrying a suspicious Skyanite shipment to Vaelorian's Adventurer's Guild, using it as cover while secretly investigating the Bloodstone's origins.

2. **Fronn Heartshield** — a human barbarian, former chief of Skyvein Hollow. He surrendered to the Iron-Blood Vanguard 7 years ago to save his daughter Elara, was tortured, sold into slavery, and spent 6 years as a gladiator ("Redfist"). After escaping and hiding in Oakhaven as a baker ("Yeast"), he was recognised by gamblers and fled south. He carries a war pick, a shield, and a desperate need to reach Skyvein Hollow.

**Initial Hook:**
A carriage carrying mysterious Skyanite cargo, a stranger in the woods, an approaching bandit attack — and the first glimpse of a truth that connects slave-trading raiders, forbidden crystals, and an ancient war against a fallen god. The party must decide who to trust and what to reveal as they approach Vaelorian's guarded gates.
