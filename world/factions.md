## The Order of the Sunspears
- **Relationship:** Allied (Varesh's former order — relationship becoming complicated)
- **Key Members:** Marshal Cassian Jaleel, Captain Idris, Liyana, Karim Ashkar
- **Goals:** Protect Elona from threats physical and supernatural; uphold Sunspear virtues (valour, humility, honour, honesty, justice, compassion, sacrifice, loyalty)
- **Resources:** Military order with influence across all three Elonian provinces; Sunspear Great Hall in Istan; training grounds, fortifications
- **Territory / Influence:** Operates across Istan, Kourna, and Vabbi; independent from provincial governments; answers to their own Spearmarshal and Command Council
- **Notes:** For Varesh, the Sunspears are not simply an army — they are a religion and the closest thing to family after Samira left. Her conflict with them is not rebellion against something she hates, but betrayal of something she loves.

## House Ashkar
- **Relationship:** Allied (family — complicated by secrets)
- **Key Members:** Karim Ashkar (patriarch), Nadijeh Ashkar (matriarch), Varesh Ashkar, Samira Ashkar (estranged/presumed dead)
- **Goals:** Maintain honour and reputation within the Sunspear Order; protect Ashkar legacy
- **Resources:** Ancient Vabbian Sunspear family; known for commanders, historians, diplomats, and Paragons
- **Territory / Influence:** Vabbi
- **Notes:** Family symbol is a golden spear crossing a desert flower. Motto: "Stand beneath the sun, carry the burden." The family has controlled its own story — including hiding the truth of Samira's fate.

## The Istani Royal Court
- **Relationship:** Friendly
- **Key Members:** Admiral Zahir al-Marin (Istani Royal Navy)
- **Goals:** Govern Istan; protect trade routes and coastal villages
- **Resources:** Istani Royal Navy; trade wealth
- **Territory / Influence:** Istan
- **Notes:** Extremely close relationship with the Sunspears. Istan is where the Sunspears are most beloved.

## The Kournan Military Council
- **Relationship:** Suspicious
- **Key Members:** [Unknown generals]
- **Goals:** Maintain order, military discipline, and agricultural stability
- **Resources:** Large standing army; fortress-city of Gandara
- **Territory / Influence:** Kourna
- **Notes:** Respectful but tense relationship with Sunspears. Values order, sacrifice, and military discipline. Kourna says: "Strength exists so weakness does not destroy us." Some Kournan commanders may secretly agree with Karim Ashkar's philosophy.

## The Vabbian Grand Princes
- **Relationship:** Friendly
- **Key Members:** The ibn Rashad family (merchant-princes)
- **Goals:** Trade, wealth accumulation, patronage of arts and scholarship
- **Resources:** Vast merchant wealth; libraries; trade caravans across Elona
- **Territory / Influence:** Vabbi
- **Notes:** Complicated relationship with Sunspears — respect them but do not always agree with their methods. House Ashkar originates from Vabbi.

## The Order of Whispers
- **Relationship:** Neutral (secretive ally)
- **Key Members:** Master Selim Voss
- **Goals:** Gather intelligence and forbidden knowledge; operate in secrecy
- **Resources:** Spy network across Elona and beyond; hidden archives
- **Territory / Influence:** All of Elona (covert)
- **Notes:** Ask the same question Varesh asks: "How much truth can the world handle?" They may have information about Samira's fate. During Nightfall, they become allies of the Sunspears.

## The Zaishen Order
- **Relationship:** Neutral
- **Key Members:** [Unknown]
- **Goals:** Religious warfare, monster hunting, divine purpose
- **Resources:** Monasteries; training grounds; divine magic
- **Territory / Influence:** Elona
- **Notes:** Religious warriors and monster hunters. May admire Varesh's power too much. Similar themes to the Sunspears: devotion, fighting supernatural threats, divine purpose.

## The Priests of Kormir
- **Relationship:** Allied
- **Key Members:** Brother Nazeem
- **Goals:** Uphold Kormir's teachings; advise Sunspear leadership; preserve divine wisdom
- **Resources:** Temples across Istan; religious authority
- **Territory / Influence:** Istan (primarily)
- **Notes:** Kormir represents sacrifice, wisdom, and hope to Varesh. After Abaddon's return, many religious figures become fearful of forbidden knowledge.

## Cults and Remnants of Abaddon
- **Relationship:** Hostile
- **Key Members:** [Unknown cult leaders]
- **Goals:** Free Abaddon; spread forbidden knowledge; undo divine restrictions
- **Resources:** Hidden temples; forbidden texts; corrupted magic
- **Territory / Influence:** The Desolation; hidden cells across Elona
- **Notes:** The enemy Varesh fears becoming. Abaddon's philosophy: knowledge should not be restricted; mortals deserve freedom; gods should not control destiny.

## The Corsairs
- **Relationship:** Neutral
- **Key Members:** [Unknown captains]
- **Goals:** Smuggling, exploration, profit
- **Resources:** Ships; knowledge of forgotten ruins and hidden routes; forbidden markets
- **Territory / Influence:** Coastal waters of Elona
- **Notes:** Criminals, smugglers, and explorers. Potentially form part of Varesh's misfit crew. They challenge Varesh: "Why do you trust institutions that lie?"

## The Centaur Tribes of Elona
- **Relationship:** Neutral
- **Key Members:** Veldrunner Centaurs
- **Goals:** Tribal survival; protect their territories
- **Resources:** Nomadic warbands; knowledge of the desert
- **Territory / Influence:** Elonian plains and deserts
- **Notes:** The Sunspears historically gained unexpected allies among groups normally considered enemies. They represent outsiders who judge by actions rather than reputation.

## The Astral Scholars of Vabbi
- **Relationship:** Friendly
- **Key Members:** [Unknown scholars]
- **Goals:** Study ancient gods, forgotten magic, and lost civilisations; translate ancient texts
- **Resources:** Libraries; scholarly networks; archaeological expeditions
- **Territory / Influence:** Vabbi
- **Notes:** A fictional academic organisation inspired by Vabbian scholarship. They help Varesh translate Abaddon's texts later in her journey.

## The Priory of Vabbi
- **Relationship:** Neutral
- **Key Members:** [Unknown researchers]
- **Goals:** Archaeology, research, history preservation
- **Resources:** Archives; excavation sites; scholarly expertise
- **Territory / Influence:** Vabbi
- **Notes:** Predecessor to the Durmand Priory. Fascinated by the Bloodstone. Unlike the Sunspears, they do not fear knowledge. Philosophy: "A secret buried is a disaster waiting to happen."

## The Iron-Blood Vanguard
- **Relationship:** Hostile
- **Key Members:** [Unknown warlord]
- **Goals:** Acquire Skyanite; conquer mountain chiefdoms; sell captives into slavery
- **Resources:** Mountain raiders; slave-trading connections; armaments
- **Territory / Influence:** Northern Crests
- **Notes:** A brutal warband of mountain raiders who devised a scheme to claim Skyvein Hollow's Skyanite. Unable to breach the symbiotic barrier, they kidnapped Fronn's daughter Elara to force his surrender. They tortured Fronn for months, then sold him into slavery when they could not extract the barrier's secret.
