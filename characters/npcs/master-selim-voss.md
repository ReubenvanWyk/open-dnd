# Master Selim Voss

**Status:** Alive
**Attitude:** Neutral (secretive)
**First met:** Background (pre-campaign)

**Race:** Human | **Class:** Whispers Agent
**Role:** Order of Whispers intelligence agent

---

## Description

Master Selim Voss is an agent of the Order of Whispers. He knew about Varesh Ossa. He knows what happened to Samira. He suspects Karim lied. But he never reveals everything because information is his weapon.

The Order of Whispers works in secrecy, gathering forbidden knowledge, and often possesses information that governments and armies do not. During the events of Nightfall, they became allies of the Sunspears after Varesh Ossa's rise.

## Relationship with Party

His relationship with Varesh follows an arc:
- **At first:** "Trust nobody."
- **Later:** "You became exactly what we feared."

## Notes

- Knows more than he reveals about the Ashkar family secrets
- Represents the moral grey area between protection and manipulation
- His faction asks the same question Varesh asks: "How much truth can the world handle?"
