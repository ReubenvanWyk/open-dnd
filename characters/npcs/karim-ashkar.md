# Karim Ashkar

**Status:** Alive
**Attitude:** Neutral (complicated)
**First met:** Background (pre-campaign)

**Race:** Human | **Class:** Sunspear Commander
**Role:** Varesh's father, House Ashkar patriarch

---

## Description

Karim Ashkar is a respected Sunspear commander who dedicated his life to protecting Elona. He is not a cruel man, nor does he hate mercy. His tragedy is that he believes mercy without consequence can become another form of failure.

Over time, Karim took one Sunspear virtue and pushed it beyond its meaning: duty became obedience, discipline became control, protection became possession. He loved his children, but he believed love meant preparing them for a world that would show them no mercy.

He intentionally gave Varesh the name Varesh (after Varesh Ossa), believing a troubled legacy could be redeemed through honourable deeds.

## Key Events

- Confronted Samira as she tried to flee the family; their argument escalated into violence
- Wounded Samira with his spear, leaving a permanent scar across her left arm
- Told the Sunspears Samira had been killed by wolves, presenting a bloodstained garment as evidence
- Gave Varesh her first Sunspear spear (the Spear of Dawn) when she joined the Order

## Notes

- His philosophy is closest to Kourna's values: order, sacrifice, strength, military discipline
- Varesh hates that she inherited his habit of slowly spinning her spear while thinking
- Always uses Varesh's full name — never a nickname
