# Oath of the Paragon — Paladin Sacred Oath

*Inspired by Guild Wars 1*

The Oath of the Paragon binds a paladin to the role of an inspiring battlefield commander. Tracing their lineage back to holy orders like the Sunspears, these paladins rely on thrown spears and commanding shouts to direct tactical maneuvers and shield their comrades. To a Paragon, true glory is never won alone, but achieved through the strength of an unbroken unit.

## Tenets of the Paragon

The tenets of the Oath of the Paragon guide a paladin's command on and off the battlefield:

- **Lead the Line.** Never ask an ally to face a danger you would not face yourself.
- **Voice of Hope.** Speak courage into the hearts of your comrades to drive back fear and despair.
- **Unity Above All.** Victory belongs to the unit. Empower your allies so you may conquer together.
- **Unyielding Vigil.** Stand as a radiant beacon against darkness, corruption, and cowardice.

## 3rd Level Features

### Spear of the Sunspear

Starting when you take this oath at 3rd level, you master mid-range combat with heavy thrown weapons:

- **Ranged Smite:** When you hit a creature with a ranged weapon attack using a weapon that has the Thrown property and lacks both the Light and Finesse properties (such as a spear or javelin), you can apply your Divine Smite feature as if it were a melee weapon attack.
- **Recall Weapon:** As a bonus action, you can magically recall one thrown weapon you possess back into your open hand, causing it to disappear from where it landed and reappear in your grasp.

### Channel Divinity

When you take this oath at 3rd level, you gain the following two Channel Divinity options:

- **Shielding Command.** As a reaction when a creature you can see within 30 feet of you is targeted by an attack roll, you can use your Channel Divinity to grant that creature a bonus to its AC equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum of +1) against that attack, potentially turning a hit into a miss.
- **Tactical Advance.** As a bonus action, you shout a tactical command. A number of willing creatures up to your Charisma modifier (minimum of 1) within 30 feet of you can immediately use their reaction to move up to half their speed without provoking opportunity attacks.

## 7th Level Feature

### Aura of Command

Starting at 7th level, you emanate an aura of tactical leadership. You and friendly creatures within your 10-foot radius aura gain the following benefits:

- A +10 foot bonus to walking speed.
- Once per turn, when a friendly creature within your aura hits with a weapon attack, they add your Charisma modifier (minimum of +1) to the damage roll.

At 18th level, the radius of this aura increases to 30 feet.

---

## 15th Level Feature

### Refrain of Leadership

Starting at 15th level, inspiring your allies re-energizes your divine light.

Whenever you cast a paladin spell of 1st level or higher that targets or heals one or more friendly creatures, you regain hit points in your Lay on Hands pool equal to twice the spell slot's level (up to your maximum pool capacity).

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum of once), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

---

## 20th Level Feature

### Avatar of the Sunspear

At 20th level, you can assume the legendary form of a Sunspear commander. As an action, you manifest glowing golden wings and radiate divine aura for 1 minute. During this time, you gain the following benefits:

- **Sun-Winged Flight:** You gain a flying speed equal to your walking speed.
- **Expanded Aura:** The radius of your Aura of Command increases to 60 feet.
- **Commanding Presence:** Whenever a friendly creature starts its turn inside your Aura of Command, it reduces all incoming damage it takes by an amount equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum of 1) until the start of its next turn.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest, unless you expend a 5th-level spell slot to use it again.
