# Locations

## Istan — The Island of Beginnings
- **Type:** Island Province
- **Region:** Elona
- **First discovered:** Pre-campaign
- **Description:** The birthplace of the Sunspears. An island of ancient ruins, old traditions, and military honour. Contains remnants of the Primeval Dynasty.
- **Government:** Istani Royal Court (capital: Kamadan)
- **Culture:** Naval, traditional, heroic

### Kamadan, Jewel of Istan
- **Type:** Capital City
- **Region:** Istan
- **First discovered:** Pre-campaign
- **Description:** The largest port city of Istan and the main entrance to Elona for foreign travellers. A place of merchants, sailors, diplomats, and soldiers.
- **Notable NPCs:** Admiral Zahir al-Marin
- **Points of interest:** Great Harbour, Sunspear gate, merchant quarters
- **Varesh connection:** Where she first experienced the wider world beyond House Ashkar; trained with other Sunspears; met foreign heroes; studied old histories

### Sunspear Great Hall
- **Type:** Military/Religious Centre
- **Region:** Istan
- **Description:** The heart of the Sunspear Order. Where new recruits train, commanders meet, victories are celebrated, and fallen Sunspears are remembered.
- **Varesh connection:** Where she became the Sunspear everyone expected her to be; where she felt the greatest pressure

### The Astralarium
- **Type:** Observatory / Centre of Learning
- **Region:** Istan
- **Description:** A place dedicated to studying the stars, history, and ancient knowledge.
- **Varesh connection:** She spent nights here studying forgotten civilizations, divine history, prophecies, and ancient texts; likely where her fascination with forbidden knowledge began

### Fahranur, The First City
- **Type:** Ancient Ruins
- **Region:** Istan
- **Description:** One of the oldest cities in Elona, dating back to earlier civilisations.
- **Varesh connection:** Perfect location for the discovery of the Bloodstone; she found evidence that history has been hidden before

### Champion's Dawn
- **Type:** Settlement
- **Region:** Istan
- **Description:** Sunspear training grounds

### Chahbek Village
- **Type:** Village
- **Region:** Istan
- **Description:** Rural Istani life

### Beknur Harbor
- **Type:** Harbour
- **Region:** Istan
- **Description:** Naval connection point

### Jokanur Diggings
- **Type:** Archaeological Site
- **Region:** Istan
- **Description:** Archaeological ruins under excavation

## Kourna — The Land of Discipline
- **Type:** Province
- **Region:** Elona
- **First discovered:** Pre-campaign
- **Description:** The southern military province of Elona. Known for its armies, agriculture, and strict devotion to order. Kournans value loyalty and service, but their culture can become rigid and authoritarian.
- **Government:** Kournan Military Council (capital: Gandara)
- **Culture:** Military, discipline, loyalty

### Gandara, The Moon Fortress
- **Type:** Fortress City
- **Region:** Kourna
- **Description:** One of the most important cities in Kourna. A massive fortress-city and symbol of military authority.
- **Varesh connection:** Where she saw the danger of pure military thinking

### Jahai Bluffs
- **Type:** Battlefield Region
- **Region:** Kourna
- **Description:** A region famous for major conflicts in Elonian history, including battles against Palawa Joko.
- **Varesh connection:** Where she studied legendary battles and began questioning how many civilians were sacrificed for victories

### Kodash Bazaar
- **Type:** Trade Centre
- **Region:** Kourna
- **Description:** A marketplace representing the civilian side of Kourna.
- **Varesh connection:** A place where she interacted with ordinary people, reminding her that nations are not only made of armies and leaders

### Pogahn Passage
- **Type:** Military Route
- **Region:** Kourna

### Yohlon Haven
- **Type:** Coastal Settlement
- **Region:** Kourna

## Vabbi — The Land of Knowledge and Nobility
- **Type:** Province
- **Region:** Elona
- **First discovered:** Pre-campaign
- **Description:** The wealthy northern province of Elona, famous for its merchant princes, luxury, gardens, scholars, and artistic achievements. One of the richest regions in Tyria.
- **Government:** Vabbian Grand Princes
- **Culture:** Wealth, art, philosophy

### Kodash Bazaar
- **Type:** Great Marketplace
- **Region:** Vabbi
- **Description:** A place of merchants, scholars, travellers, and rare artefacts.
- **Varesh connection:** Where she met Jaleh ibn Rashad; where she learned that life is not only duty

### Garden of Seborhin
- **Type:** Vabbian Gardens
- **Region:** Vabbi
- **Description:** A symbol of Vabbian luxury and beauty.
- **Varesh connection:** Where she secretly relaxes, sketches plants, writes poetry, and practises calligraphy. Represents the life she could have had.

### The Veiled Desert
- **Type:** Wilderness / Ancient Mysteries
- **Region:** Vabbi
- **Description:** A place filled with forgotten ruins and hidden archives.

### Jennur's Horde
- **Type:** Ancient Ruins
- **Region:** Vabbi
- **Description:** Ancient ruins and hidden secrets

### The Mirror of Lyss
- **Type:** Religious/Philosophical Site
- **Region:** Vabbi

### Mihanu Township
- **Type:** Civilian Settlement
- **Region:** Vabbi

## The Desolation — The Wound of Elona
- **Type:** Wasteland
- **Region:** Elona (between Elona and the Realm of Torment)
- **First discovered:** Pre-campaign
- **Description:** A corrupted wasteland shaped by ancient magic, demons, and the legacy of Abaddon.
- **Culture:** Survival (no government)
- **Symbol:** The consequences of forbidden power

### The Bone Palace
- **Type:** Fortress
- **Region:** The Desolation
- **Description:** Fortress of Palawa Joko. A symbol of undead rule.
- **Varesh connection:** A reminder that tyrants often claim they are bringing order

### Crystal Desert Border
- **Type:** Border Territory
- **Region:** The Desolation
- **Description:** Ancient Forgotten territory connected to the Forgotten and Ascension.
- **Varesh connection:** Where she researched gods, magic, ascension, and Bloodstones

---

## The Realm of Torment
- **Type:** Divine Prison / Plane
- **Region:** Connected to Abaddon
- **First discovered:** Pre-campaign (knowledge)
- **Description:** The domain connected to Abaddon and the Margonites. Represents divine punishment, forgotten history, and corrupted knowledge.
- **Varesh connection:** Where she discovered that the enemy she feared may have been more complicated than history claimed

---

## House Ashkar Estate
- **Type:** Noble Estate
- **Region:** Vabbi
- **First discovered:** Pre-campaign
- **Description:** The ancestral home of House Ashkar, one of the old Vabbian Sunspear families who produced generals and historians.
- **Family Symbol:** A golden spear crossing a desert flower
- **Family Motto:** "Stand beneath the sun, carry the burden"
- **Notable NPCs:** Karim Ashkar, Nadijeh Ashkar, Varesh Ashkar, Samira Ashkar
