## Description

Elara is Fronn Heartshield's daughter. Her mother Rowena died in childbirth. She was raised by Fronn and the community of Skyvein Hollow.

At age 11, she was ambushed by the Iron-Blood Vanguard outside the symbiotic barrier of Skyvein Hollow and used as leverage to force her father's surrender.

After Fronn handed himself over, Elara was entrusted to the care of Fronn's sister and mother.

## Relationship with Party

Fronn Heartshield's daughter. His goal is to reunite with her.
